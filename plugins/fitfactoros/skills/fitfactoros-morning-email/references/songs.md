Rules for this list:
- Title and artist only. Never quote lyrics in the email.
- Well-known songs that are easy to find on YouTube, Spotify, or Apple Music.
- Nothing explicit, religious, or political.
- Themes come from this list only: starting, persistence, craft, connection, courage, patience, rest, momentum, focus, curiosity.

The email links to a YouTube search for "title artist," so a link never goes stale.
