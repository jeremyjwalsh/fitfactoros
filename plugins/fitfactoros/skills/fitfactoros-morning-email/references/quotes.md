# Quotes for the morning email

Rules for this list:
- Copy each quote exactly as written here. Never reword it, shorten it, or change the credit.
- Only old, well-documented sources. A quote goes in only after a person checks its exact wording against a source. If it can't be confirmed, it stays out.
- No religious texts (see the guardrails).
- Themes come from this list only: starting, persistence, craft, connection, courage, patience, rest, momentum, focus, curiosity.

"Checked" says how the wording was confirmed. Every row here was checked on the date shown. A new quote goes in only with its own check.
