# Quick screen rubrics

Five factors, each scored 1–10, then weighted to a Fit score out of 100.

**Fit score = (Role match × 3.5) + (Work you want × 2.5) + (Level and pay × 1.5) + (Stability × 1.5) + (Pull × 1.0)**

Round to a whole number.

## Scoring rules
- **5 means typical.** Most jobs and most companies are a 5 on most factors. That's not a bad score; it's an honest one.
- **Not verified caps at 6.** If a factor rests on an Inferred claim or a guess, it can't score above 6.
- **9–10 needs a verified reason,** named in the Why line.
- **If more than 2 factors score 9 or higher, re-check them.** That's usually generous scoring, not a perfect job.
- **A skipped Profile answer caps the factor that depends on it at 6.**

## 1. Role match (weight 3.5)
Compare the posting's must-haves with the Profile: most recent role (B5), years (B6), top skills (B7), target roles (B8).
- **9–10:** The title is one of their target roles, and the Profile shows every must-have.
- **6–8:** Meets most must-haves; one gap that's close to what they've done.
- **5:** Meets about half. Or the posting is too vague to tell.
- **1–4:** Misses a hard must-have (a license, years in a specific field, managing people they haven't managed), or the title is far from their targets.

## 2. Work you want (weight 2.5)
Compare the day-to-day duties with the work that makes them lose track of time (B13) and their target roles (B8).
- **9–10:** The core duties are the work they named in B13.
- **6–8:** Some of the duties are that work.
- **5:** Neutral, or the posting doesn't describe the day-to-day.
- **1–4:** The day-to-day is mostly work they didn't name, or the opposite of it.

## 3. Level and pay (weight 1.5)
Compare the level with their experience, and the posted pay with their pay floor (B10).
- **9–10:** Posted pay is clearly above the floor, and the level matches their experience.
- **6–8:** Pay is at or near the floor, or the level is one notch off.
- **5:** Pay not posted and the level fits. Don't guess the pay.
- **1–4:** Clearly too junior or a big stretch in level.

## 4. Stability (weight 1.5)
From the company check (Step 4 in SKILL.md).
- **9–10:** Verified: public and profitable, or recently well funded, with no layoffs in the last 12 months.
- **6–8:** Funded and growing, with some mixed news.
- **5:** Typical, or not enough found to say.
- **1–4:** Layoffs in the last 12 months (especially in this team's area), money trouble, or heavy leadership turnover.

## 5. Pull (weight 1.0)
- **9:** The company is on their dream companies list (B14).
- **7:** The user said in this chat that they're excited about it.
- **5:** Everything else.

## Screen result bands

| Fit score | Screen result |
|---|---|
| 70 or higher | Pursue |
| 55–69 | Maybe |
| Below 55 | Pass |

Overrides:
- Any gate ✗ → **Pass**, Fit score `n/a`.
- A gate ? on location or a deal-breaker → **Maybe** at most.
- No Profile, or a company-only screen → **Maybe** at most.

These are starting bands, set before real-world testing. If the weights change, re-test the bands on a handful of real jobs. Weights and bands work as one system.
