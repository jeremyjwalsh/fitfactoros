# FitFactorOS cheat sheet

Say these in a new chat inside your FitFactorOS Project. Plain words work; you don't need to type them exactly.

## When something happens (same day is best)

| Say this | What you get |
|---|---|
| **log this** + a sentence or two ("Had the Northwind interview today, felt solid") | Activity rows to paste, and the one Jobs cell to change if a step moved |
| **I applied to…** / **I had a call with…** | Same as log this |
| **check my week** (or "what did I miss?") | Claude checks your calendar and email, read-only, lists what looks like job-search activity you haven't logged, and asks about each one. Only what you confirm becomes a row. |

Do it the same day. The details are still fresh, and the morning email stops nudging you about something you already did.

## When you find a job

| Say this | What you get |
|---|---|
| **fit factor this** + the job link | Company fit, then Role fit. You decide: pursue, park as a maybe, or pass |
| **go deep** | Deeper research on a job you're serious about |

## End of the week (Saturday or Sunday)

| Say this | What you get |
|---|---|
| **this week's work search log** | Your weekly log in your state's format, ready to copy into your certification |
| **did I do enough this week?** | Your count against your state's weekly minimum |

If Google Sheets is connected, Claude reads your **Activity** and **Jobs** tabs itself (read-only). If not, copy them into the chat when asked; the whole tabs are fine and Claude picks out the week. If Gmail or Google Calendar is connected, Claude offers to check them for anything you missed first. You also get rows for any jobs you screened but haven't logged yet.

## Any time

| Say this | What you get |
|---|---|
| **show my dashboard** (or "where do I stand?") | Your top picks, ladders, and this week, on a private page |
| **morning email** | Today's email draft, right now |
| **update my profile** (or "change my pay floor," "add a contact") | Changes one answer |

## Setup and settings

| Say this | What you get |
|---|---|
| **start my intake** | Builds your Profile (about 10 minutes) |
| **set up my morning email** / **change my morning email** / **stop my morning email** | Turns the weekday draft on, changes it, or turns it off |

## Good to know

- **You send everything.** Claude drafts and gives you rows to paste. It never sends, submits, or posts.
- **Morning email on your phone:** Gmail can't open links inside a draft. Tap the button on your computer, or send the draft to yourself first.
- **The email repeats a move until your Sheet changes.** Done already? Say **log this** and update the Next step in your Jobs tab.
