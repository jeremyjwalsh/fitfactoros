# Observer sheet: first-time setup test

**Your job is to watch, not help.** Print this page. One observer, one tester, about 45 minutes.

## Before you start
- Tester uses **their own computer and browser**, signed out of Claude (or a brand-new account).
- Give them only two things: the **README link** (github.com/jeremyjwalsh/fitfactoros) and **one job link**.
- Say only this: *"This is a free tool for job searching. See if you can get it to tell you whether this job is a good fit for you. Please think out loud. I'm testing the instructions, not you, and I can't help unless you're really stuck."*
- Then stay quiet. If they ask you something, say: *"What would you try?"* Help only if they're about to quit, and write down that you did.

## Watch for
**Hesitate (H):** pauses 10+ seconds, scrolls up and down, rereads.
**Ask (A):** asks you or says "I don't know what this means."
**Nearly quit (Q):** sighs, says "forget it," walks away, or you step in.
**Trust (T):** says they're uneasy about a permission, a download, or an account.

## Ask at the end (2 minutes)
1. "Where was it hardest?"
2. "Was there a moment you almost stopped?"
3. "Would you use the fit result? What was it worth?"
4. "Would you trust it with your Google account? Why or why not?"
