# FitFactorOS brand

One page on how FitFactorOS looks and how to write about it. Keep it plain and honest, not hype.

## Names

FitFactorOS has three levels:

| Level | Name | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Parent brand | **FitFactorOS** | What every edition shares: the honest tags, the guardrails, and "you decide." |
| Edition | **Jobs** (now), **College** (planned) | Each edition has its own questions and scoring. |
| Way in | **Fit Check** and **Fit Track** | Fit Check is the quick read (one paste, nothing saved). Fit Track is the full system. Every edition has both. |

**How to write them**
- Editions: "FitFactorOS Jobs", "FitFactorOS College". No dash, no "for".
- The ways in: "FitFactorOS | Fit Check" and "FitFactorOS | Fit Track". Never "Fit Check" or "Fit Track" alone in public material: on their own they read like fitness apps, and other tools already use the name Fit Check.
- Where both editions appear together, use the full form: "FitFactorOS Jobs | Fit Check".
- Spell out FitFactorOS. "FFOS" is private shorthand only.

**What it is:** a **job fit system**, not a job search system. It doesn't find jobs or apply for you; it helps you evaluate them and follow through on the ones worth it. ("Job search" is fine when it means the person's own search.)

**Lines**
- One-liner: *Find jobs worth pursuing. Know why they're a fit.*
- Descriptor: *A free, open-source job fit system for Claude.*
- Tagline: *FitFactorOS helps you make better decisions. Fit Check helps you decide. Fit Track helps you follow through.*

**Say what's true.** FitFactorOS never sends, submits, or posts anything on its own; the person does. Don't write copy that suggests otherwise.

## Logo

Three stacked bars, one for each tag (Verified, Estimated, Inferred), then "FitFactor" in Fraunces with "OS" in IBM Plex Sans, accent blue, and a small ™. Edition versions add a thin gray divider and the edition name.

| File | Use |
|---|---|

| [fitfactoros-jobs.png](fitfactoros-jobs.png) | FitFactorOS Jobs |
| [fitfactoros-college.png](fitfactoros-college.png) | FitFactorOS College (for later) |

Transparent PNGs, for light backgrounds. Use the logo as a picture, never retyped as text: in slides and documents, other computers swap the fonts and the text version breaks.

These match the dashboard and morning email.

## Type

- **Headings:** Fraunces
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans
- **Tags and the paste line:** IBM Plex Mono

All three are free Google Fonts. Presenting slides on someone else's computer? Use basic fonts (for example Georgia and Arial) for the text and keep the logo as a picture.

## The name and the credit

## Examples

Use only made-up people and companies: Alex Sample, Priya, Fabrikam, Northwind, Tailspin Labs, and the like. Never real people, real employers, or real pay from anyone's search.
